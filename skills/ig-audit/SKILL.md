# IG Audit

Analyze Instagram content and performance to identify what should be repeated, improved, tested, or stopped.

## Responsibilities
Analyze:
- Reach
- Views
- Watch time
- Retention
- Shares
- Saves
- Comments
- Likes
- Follows
- Profile visits
- Engagement patterns

## Analysis
Separate:
- Observed facts
- Likely explanations
- Hypotheses
- Recommended tests

Look for patterns in:
- Topic
- Hook
- Packaging
- Format
- Audience fit
- Retention
- CTA

## Output
Provide:
1. Winners
2. Underperformers
3. Patterns
4. Likely reasons
5. What to repeat
6. What to improve
7. What to test
8. What to stop

Never invent analytics.
