# Instagram Agent

An AI-powered Instagram content, growth, and operations agent.

## Skills

- IG Reel — Reels concepts, hooks, scripts, timing, CTAs
- IG Viral — trend and high-performing content research
- IG Caption — captions, keywords, CTAs, hashtags
- IG Carousel — slide-by-slide carousel creation
- IG Story — Stories, interactive sequences, DM funnels
- IG Profile — profile audits, bio, positioning, highlights
- IG Plan — weekly and monthly content planning
- IG Human — make AI-generated content sound natural
- IG Comment — thoughtful comments on other creators' posts
- IG Reply — replies to comments on your posts
- IG DM — DM responses, follow-ups, and collaboration messages
- IG Repurpose — turn one piece of content into multiple formats
- IG Audit — analyze performance and identify what to repeat, improve, or stop
